Convert Wh to W (watthours to watts)

To convert Wh to W, you need to divide the number of Watthours by the number of hours:
First, fill in this box with your Wh:
Wh
Then fill in this box with the duration in hours and click on Calculate
Duration: hours
Watts
The formula is (Wh)/(h) = (W). For example, if you have 100 Wh for a duration of 2 hours, then the wattage is (100)/(2) = (50) Watts.
(Watthours is a measure of energy and watts is a unit of power. Power multiplied by time is enery).
