Convert A to mA (Amps to Milliamps)

To convert A to mA, you need to multiply the number of Amps by 1000:
Fill in this box with your Amps and click on Calculate to obtain Milliamps:
A
mA
The formula is (A)*(1000) = (mA). For example, if you have 2 A, then the number of Milliamps is (1000)*(2) = (2000) mA.

Convert mA to A (Milliamps to Amps)

To convert mA to A, you need to divide the number of mA by 1000:
Fill in this box with your mA and click on Calculate to obtain A:
mA
A
The formula is (mA)/(1000) = (A). For example, if you have 2000 mA, then the number of Amps is (2000)/(1000) = (2) A.
