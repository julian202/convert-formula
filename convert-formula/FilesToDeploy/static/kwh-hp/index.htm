Convert kilowatt-hour (kWh) to horsepower (hp)

Note: this page converts kWh to hp, if instead you want to convert kW to hp click here.
To convert kWh to hp you need to know the total number of hours that it was running (for example, the maximum number of hours that a battery can run on one charge). Enter the kWh and hours below and click on Calculate to obtain hp:
kWh
hours
hp
The formula is (kWh)*(1.3404825737)/(hours) = (hp). For example, if you have 200 kWh that runs for 20 hours, the power is (200)*(1.3404825737)/(20) = 13.404825 hp.

The formula is (kWh)= (hp)*(hours)/(1.3404825737). For example, if you have 100 hp that runs for 20 hours, the kWh is (100)*(20)/(1.3404825737) = 1492 kWh.
