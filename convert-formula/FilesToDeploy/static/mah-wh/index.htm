How to convert mAh to Watt hours

Insert milliamp hour (mAh) and voltage (V) and click on Calculate to obtain Watt hours (Wh).

The formula is (mAh)*(V)/1000 = (Wh). For example, if you have a 300mAh battery rated at 5V, you obtain 300mAh * 5V / 1000 = 1.5Wh.
